Getting here from Helsinki Airport

Pajulahti is in Nastola, Lahti, 115 km from Helsinki Airport. Kisakallio is in Lohja, 64 km from the airport.

  • By car or coach: About an hour and a half from Helsinki Airport to Pajulahti. Parking at Pajulahti is free.
  • By train and bus: You can reach Lahti travel centre (Lahden matkakeskus), a railway and bus station, by train. [VAHVISTA: 1 h 15 min from airport] From there, bus 11 runs to Pajulahti once an hour. The journey takes about 35 minutes. [VAHVISTA: valid from 1.1.2027]
  • Group transfer: Ask for a coach from Helsinki Airport to Pajulahti and back, and we include it in your quote. [VAHVISTA: from €780 per coach]
  • Kisakallio (curling camps): About an hour by car from Helsinki Airport. Kisakallio cannot be reached conveniently by public transport, so plan to come by car or coach. [VAHVISTA: from €720 per coach]

Camps and prices

Each camp has two prices: the first includes Finnish VAT, the second excludes it. The price depends on the group size and the services you choose. The final price is confirmed in the quote.

Accommodation, meals and admission to events take place in Finland, so Finnish VAT applies to them whether the buyer is Finnish or foreign. Some other services sold to an organisation with an EU VAT number, such as coaching or testing, may fall under the reverse-charge rule instead. The first price includes Finnish VAT on every item and is the one to budget with; the second shows the amount before VAT. Your quote shows the VAT treatment of each item, including facility use and transfers. [VAHVISTA: approved 15.12.2026]

  • There is an induction loop at reception and in some rooms.

Training centre (our English page)

What are the altitude rooms, and who can use them?

Pajulahti has hypoxic alpine huts and an altitude training room at 1,800 m in Puistopaju C. Altitude training loads the body more than training in normal conditions and may involve risks. We offer altitude blocks to athletes only, and they suit athletes with a solid training background. [VAHVISTA]

Is Pajulahti accessible for para athletes?

Yes. All Pajulahti sports facilities are accessible. Kalliopaju hotel is fully accessible, with braille signs and a covered walkway to the main building, and Puistopaju has 16 accessible rooms. There is an induction loop at reception and in some rooms. Pajulahti hosts top-level para-sport camps. Tell us your athletes' accessibility needs in your request, and the quote will show the rooms and facilities we propose.

Can you cater for special diets?

Yes. Special diets are taken into account. Tell us about them in your request.

Can we hold dates provisionally?

Yes. We hold the dates provisionally, so that your head coach and finance can confirm the camp. A provisional hold is free of charge and valid for 14 days. [VAHVISTA]

What are the cancellation terms?

A booking is binding once we have confirmed it in writing, and the programme and list of names are due 14 days before arrival. For groups of under 50 people, we charge a handling fee of €75 if a confirmed booking is cancelled. If it is cancelled 21–8 days before arrival, we charge 50 % of the booking value, and if 7–0 days before, the full value. [VAHVISTA: valid for 2027] Your quote states the terms for your camp.
